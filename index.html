About Us

EBM was known as Employee Benefits Management from 2008 to 2016, then transformed to Effective Business Management in 2017 as the business and services rapidly growing.

WHY USE EBM?

Our distinguishing feature is that we are service-driven and strive at all times as a One Stop Service For Investors (local and international companies), ranging from company registration, accounting & tax filing, recruitment service, payroll outsourcing, company secretarial, statutory compliance, to skill development.

+ VISION

To deliver excellent and value-added professional service to our clients.

+ MISSION

Committed and passionate in providing professional and high-quality service on time. Build lasting relationships through our fairness in all deals. Provide customized and flexible professional services.

+ CORE VALUES

Excellence, Integrity, Accountability, Professionalism, Continuous Improvement.
